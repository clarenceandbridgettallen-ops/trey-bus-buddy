### Remaining blockers and next safe step
1. Parse and preserve all official frequency rows and service-date applicability; support headway windows without inventing exact departures, and distinguish unaffected fixed-time trips.
2. Confirm physical missing stops, actual walking paths/crossings and correct boarding sides, especially ambiguous same-name stops.
3. Verify two-plus-transfer timing, accessible mobile controls, TalkBack, spoken navigation and an entire physical start-to-destination journey. Tracker data are undecoded, so no live arrival estimates.
4. Direct container DNS could not fetch the official ZIP, and the public Pages URL was not accessible to the web extractor. GitHub Actions can download and inspect the ZIP; CI is not a substitute for interactive Android testing.

**Rider safety conclusion:** The app is not yet independently-travel-ready. A mocked passing test is not a real journey, and a successful Pages deployment is not proof of Android behavior.
