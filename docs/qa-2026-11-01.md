### Verified issue and incremental repair
- In the actual GPS reverse-geocoder, the address label unconditionally appended `South Carolina` even if the geocoding provider returned an address in North Carolina or no street address. This could invent an incorrect state or present a city-only result as a precise location, especially near the geographic boundary.
- [App fix 5300e35](https://github.com/clarenceandbridgettallen-ops/trey-bus-buddy/commit/5300e35ccc64117f69903f9ed0caf36b3730b85c) now preserves the geocoder's actual state, city and postcode, and falls back to explicitly labeled GPS coordinates when no street-level address is returned. The rider must still confirm their starting location. No guess about a pedestrian route, bus arrival or boarding side was added.
- Independently executed the **actual extracted reverse-geocoding function** with mocked provider responses: a North Carolina street remained North Carolina; an empty provider address returned coordinates marked `street address unavailable`; a valid Georgetown street retained South Carolina. All three isolated cases passed.
- [Regression commit 2ead5a9](https://github.com/clarenceandbridgettallen-ops/trey-bus-buddy/commit/2ead5a936e2eed7db73ac3432be035997481e67c) adds these three mocked cases to the existing full journey regression.
