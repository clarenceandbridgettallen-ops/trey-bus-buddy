This was dangerous for an accessibility-focused navigation tool.
- **After:** Commit `2c265606b76c49ed12cddbe0ceb238e67677f677` removes the generic-stop fallback, refuses to recommend a stop without official loaded GTFS stop data, and displays an actionable retry/Coast RTA contact message. The welcome-screen data-load status is visible and announces changes to assistive technology.

Three focused synthetic regression checks passed: missing official data blocks recommendation, a nearby official route-associated stop is selectable, and a distant official stop is rejected.
