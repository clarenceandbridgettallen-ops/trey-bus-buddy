Trey's original trip guide

Important: Bus Buddy prefers official Coast RTA GTFS stop data when successfully published, otherwise it uses community map data. Neither source independently verifies the physical bus-stop sign or current service. A mapped stop may be outdated, not served, or on the wrong side of the road. Never cross an unsafe road to reach a stop. Confirm service and direction before boarding.
