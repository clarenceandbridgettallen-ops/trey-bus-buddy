Important: Nearby stops currently come from OpenStreetMap community mapping, not the complete Coast RTA stop database. The official GTFS stop feed is being investigated and is not yet integrated. A mapped stop may be outdated, not served, or on the wrong side of the road. Never cross an unsafe road to reach a stop. Confirm service and direction before boarding.
